Locklang Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Türkçe

Locklang is a language-learning app published by Doğukan Tezcan. It works entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Locklang has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to any server operated by us.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings (the language you speak, the language you are learning, your level and daily goal).
  • Your learning progress (which words and sentences you have studied and when they are due for review).

This data stays on your device, in the app's own storage, and is shared only with the Locklang widgets on the same device so they can show your words. It is removed when you delete the app. If you use iCloud or computer backups of your device, the data is included in those backups under Apple's terms.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No push notification service is used and no device token is collected.

Speech

Pronunciation uses the speech voices built into iOS. Text is spoken on your device.

Children

The app contains no content unsuitable for children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
